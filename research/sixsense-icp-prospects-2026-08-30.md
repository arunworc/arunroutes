# SixSense (sixsense.ai) — ICP & Client Prospect List

*Researched 30 Aug 2026 via Exa (company site, TechCrunch, SEMI, LinkedIn,
trade press). Purpose: application artifact for the Founding GTM Engineer

---

## 1. Company snapshot

- **What they sell:** AI platform for semiconductor manufacturing —
  AI-ADC (automated defect classification: Build AI / ClassifAI / VerifAI),
  Lot Disposition & Root-Cause Analysis (DetectAI / TraceAI / CausifAI),
  and Predict AI (predictive inspection sampling).
- **Proof points they cite:** 90%+ reduction in manual inspection, 1–2%
  yield gain, 30% faster cycle time, 0% escapes, 100M+ chips processed.
- **HQ/footprint:** Singapore HQ, founded 2018 (Akanksha Jagwani CEO,
  Avni Agarwal CTO); ~38 people across Singapore, India, US, Taiwan.
  $12.2M raised; $8.5M round Jul 2025.
- **Named customers/partners:** GlobalFoundries (since 2022; gave them an
  AI Innovation Award 2025), Infineon Singapore (early adopter), JCET /
  STATS ChipPAC, Tong Hsing (TW assembly & test, Feb 2026), Raytek (TW
  advanced packaging), Wavetek (UMC specialty-foundry subsidiary),
  DenseLight (SG photonics).
- **Integration surface:** inspection tools from KLA, AMAT, Onto, Camtek
  (~60% of installed market), Klarf formats, DMS/MES write-back.
- **2026 roadmap:** real-time in-machine ADC with equipment OEMs; expanding
  from ADC into deviation control (Test, Metrology, FDC).

## 2. ICP

**CEO's own words (TechCrunch, Jul 2025):** "Our target customers are
large-scale chipmakers — foundries, OSATs, and IDMs… We're already working
with fabs in Singapore, Malaysia, Taiwan, and Israel, and are now expanding
into the U.S." Plus the stated tailwind: greenfield fabs/OSATs in Malaysia,
Singapore, Vietnam, India, US — "starting fresh, without legacy systems…
far more open to AI-native approaches from day one."

**Firmographics**
- Foundry (specialty/mature node, 100–300mm), OSAT / assembly & test /
  advanced packaging, or IDM with in-house fab or backend.
- High-volume optical/SEM inspection lines with human defect-review teams
  (that's the labor SixSense removes).
- High-mix production (hundreds of devices/defect classes — where their
  single-model foundation-model pitch lands hardest).
- Automotive / RF / medical quality regimes (zero-escape requirements).
- Regions in order of fit: Taiwan, Malaysia, Singapore, India, Vietnam,
  US, Israel; China possible but geopolitically slower.

**Buyer personas**
- Economic: VP Ops / Fab GM / Site Director; Head of Quality & Yield.
- Champion: Defect/Yield Engineering Manager (product is built for them —
  no-code, deploy in <2 days without data scientists).
- Influencers: Smart Manufacturing / Industry 4.0 / Digital Transformation
  lead; CIO.

**Trigger signals (what makes an account "now")**
- New plant groundbreaking/inauguration or capacity expansion.
- Smart-factory / Industry-4.0 / AI-initiative announcements.
- Mass hiring for operators/inspection (labor cost = automation budget).
- Automotive-grade qualification pushes; quality-escape incidents.
- SEMICON exhibitor lists; installed KLA/AMAT/Onto/Camtek base.

**Anti-ICP (don't waste cycles)**
- Fabless/design houses (no factory), pure test-only houses (limited
  visual inspection), tiny R&D/pilot lines.
- Leading-edge giants with in-house AI platforms at scale (TSMC, Samsung,
  Intel, SK Hynix) — multi-year long shots, not pipeline.

## 4. How to use this in the application

- The pitch: "I built your target-account list before you hired me." Lead
  with Tier A — a Bengaluru-based GTM engineer covering Tata (Vemagal is
  effectively local), Micron Sanand, CG Semi, Kaynes and HCL–Foxconn is a
  territory argument, not just a hire.
- The GTM-engineering angle: these accounts are enumerable (~200 fabs/OSATs
  worldwide fit the ICP). This is a named-account motion — signal tracking
  (groundbreakings, SEMICON lists, hiring surges, tool installs) beats
  volume outbound. That's precisely a GTM engineer's job description.
- Caveats: built from public sources in ~30 min; before using numbers in
  outreach, re-verify per repo rule #1. Existing-customer statuses (Tower?
  Israel; UMC parent) are inferences to check on a first call, not claims.
