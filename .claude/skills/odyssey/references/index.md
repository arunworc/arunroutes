# Index to the Odyssey (Butler translation)

The poem is divided into 24 “books” (chapters). The full text of each is in
`text/book-01.md` … `text/book-24.md`. Each file opens with Butler’s own
argument (chapter summary) in italics, then the prose text. Bracketed numbers
like `[88]` are footnote markers resolved in `footnotes.md`.

## Structure at a glance

- **Books I–IV — the “Telemachy.”** Ithaca overrun by suitors; Telemachus travels to Pylos and Sparta for news of his father. The story opens *in medias res*, in the tenth year of Ulysses’ wanderings.
- **Books V–VIII — arrival among the Phaeacians.** Calypso releases Ulysses; shipwreck; Nausicaa; the court of Alcinous.
- **Books IX–XII — the wanderings, told in flashback** by Ulysses at Alcinous’ banquet (Cyclops, Circe, the dead, Sirens, Scylla, the cattle of the Sun).
- **Books XIII–XVI — return to Ithaca** in disguise; reunion with Telemachus at Eumaeus’ hut.
- **Books XVII–XXIV — the revenge.** The beggar in the hall, the bow contest, the killing of the suitors, reunion with Penelope, peace.
