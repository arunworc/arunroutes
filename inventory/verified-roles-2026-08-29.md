## Honest framing for outreach

Say this, not more than this:

- These are **verified-open postings**, not warm intros. Do not imply a
  relationship with the hiring company that does not exist.
- Do not attach dollar figures to past results that cannot be evidenced.
- The offer is: *"I will go at the hiring manager directly and try to turn one
  of these into a real conversation for you."* That is deliverable and true.

## Claims to stop using

The July Instantly campaign copy carried two proof lines:
- "Recently connected a recruiting firm that closed 20 placements in 55 days."
- "Recently helped a recruiting firm add $105K in a single quarter."

No supporting record for either was found in the account. Retire them or
substantiate them before they go out again.
